## What evidence would make the claims stronger?

The strongest evidence for a finished supplement would come from well-designed human studies that test the exact formula at the labelled dose, report meaningful outcomes, and make their methods and limitations available for scrutiny. Research on one ingredient, a different dose, a different preparation, or an animal model cannot automatically establish the effect of a multi-ingredient product.

Transparency also matters. Consumers should be able to identify the ingredients, understand the amount in each serving, review warnings, and find credible information about quality controls. If a blend lists a combined total without showing individual quantities, it is harder to compare those amounts with the studies cited in marketing. Absence of public evidence does not prove a product cannot work; it means the claim has not been adequately demonstrated.
